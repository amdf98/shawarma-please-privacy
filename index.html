Privacy Policy – Shawarma Please

Last Updated: November 24, 2026

Thank you for playing Shawarma Please.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle user information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Shawarma Please does not directly collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

However, the Game may use third-party services that automatically collect limited technical information required for app functionality and improvement. This information may include:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system version
  • Anonymous device identifiers
  • Gameplay statistics
  • Crash reports and performance data
  • Advertising ID (if ads are enabled)

If you choose to sign in to save your progress in the cloud, an anonymous player ID and your game progress are stored with Unity Gaming Services.

This data is collected anonymously and cannot be used to identify you personally.

2. Use of Information

Collected information may be used to:

  • Improve game performance and stability
  • Fix bugs and crashes
  • Analyze gameplay experience
  • Save and restore your game progress
  • Deliver advertisements (if applicable)
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse

We do not sell, trade, or rent personal information to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

The Game may use trusted third-party services such as:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob (for advertisements)
  • Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads (for advertisements)
  • Unity Gaming Services (for sign-in and cloud save)
  • Google Firebase (analytics and crash reporting)

These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing:

  • Google’s Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity’s Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

4. Permissions

Shawarma Please only requests permissions necessary for normal game operation. The Game does not access contacts, photos or media files, microphone, camera, or location data, unless explicitly required by platform services.

5. Device and Network Security

We do not intentionally collect sensitive device data or interfere with device functionality. The Game does not engage in malicious behavior, deceptive practices, or unauthorized device or network access.

6. Children’s Privacy

Shawarma Please is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

7. Data Retention

Anonymous technical data may be retained only as long as necessary to improve the Game and maintain service functionality. You can delete your cloud save and account at any time from the game settings.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
