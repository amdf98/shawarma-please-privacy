Delete your Shawarma Please account and data

Option 1 – in the game

Open Shawarma Please, go to Settings, open your account and choose "Delete account". This deletes your player account and your cloud save straight away.

Option 2 – by email

Send an email to UBTOPstudio@gmail.com with the subject "Shawarma Please account deletion" from the email address you want us to reply to. We delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your player account (anonymous player ID) and sign-in link.
  • Your cloud save (game progress, purchase records kept in the cloud).

What is kept

  • Anonymous analytics and crash data that cannot be linked to you, for up to 14 months (analytics) and 90 days (crash reports).
  • Google Play keeps its own purchase records under Google's policies.

Game progress saved only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the game.
